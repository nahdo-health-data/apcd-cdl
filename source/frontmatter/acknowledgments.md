# Acknowledgments

Development of the APCD Common Data Layout (APCD-CDL™) was a collaborative effort of the APCD Council, individuals representing APCDs, APCD contractors and vendors, and data submitters.

The California Department of Health Care Access and Information (HCAI) and Freedman HealthCare developed the Expanded Non-Claims Payment Framework (or Expanded Framework) in consultation with Bailit Health, the author of a Millbank Memorial Fund paper categorizing non-claims-based primary care spending.[^1][^2] This guidance adds to the Expanded Framework two values for capturing the fee-for-service payments and the member count.

[^1]: V. Pegany, M. Brandt, N. Tran, M. Valle, and C. Krawcyzk, A New Standard for Categorizing and Collecting Non-Claims Payment Data, March 2024, https://www.milbank.org/2024/03/a-new-standard-for-categorizing-and-collecting-non-claims-payment-data/ (accessed March 2024).
[^2]: E. Taylor, M. Bailit, and D. Kanneganti, Measuring the Prevalence and Drivers of Non-Claims-Based Care in Commercially Insured Populations, Milbank Memorial Fund, April 2021, https://www.milbank.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/Measuring_Non-Claims_7-1.pdf (accessed March 2024).
