# Purpose

The purpose of the Common Data Layout (CDL) for All-Payer Claims Databases (APCD-CDL™) and its Non-Claims Payment (NCP) Data Layout is to harmonize healthcare payments and member eligibility data collection efforts across states. This unified framework aims to reduce administrative costs and improve the accuracy and efficiency of claims-based and non-claims payment data collection.

State APCD programs determine submission requirements, such as the scope of plans required to submit and which files are required, member inclusion criteria, submission frequency, and due dates, including how much run-out will be allowed and whether to refresh previous years’ data.
